# XIX — Build Document 3: The App, the Rest of the Features, and TestFlight

**Version:** 1.0 · 12 September 2026  
**Depends on:** v0.3.0-app (XIXScoring, XIXModels, XIXData, XIXUI; migrations 0001–0017), Build Docs 1 v1.3 and 2 v1.1, PRD v0.3, Claude Design Passes 5–7  
**Scope:** everything between v0.3.0-app and a TestFlight build a foursome can play a real round on. Ordered so the app is playable end to end as early as possible, then completed feature by feature.

---

## Part A — Principles for this document

- **Playable first.** Step 1 produces an app that can create a round, join by link, play the hole card, and end at a results screen — with static stickers and no paywall. Everything after that is additive.
- **Same rhythm:** one step, one commit, stop for review. Each step names its acceptance.
- **No new visual ideas.** Every screen exists in Passes 5–7; this document maps them to code.
- **The vocabulary sweep stays on.** No bet/wager/stakes/owe/settle/cash anywhere, including StoreKit product names and TestFlight notes.
- **MMM is read, never written,** and never restyled.

---

## Part B — Steps

### Step 1 — The app target and the playable loop

- App target `XIX` (SwiftUI lifecycle, iOS 17+), composing the packages. Environment: local Supabase by default in Debug, shared project in Release, switchable in a debug menu.
- **Sign in with Apple** wired to `AuthService`; requested lazily at the first identity-needing action.
- **Home** (Pass 6 §1): "Same as last time" card, New round, Solo round, personal record for the last course (from local history; rankings later).
- **Round setup** (Pass 6 §1, five screens): course search with recents and "use as new course"; par chips (default 4, "Skip par" with the greyed-games explanation); players with recents and "Has XIX / By name"; games picker as the opt-in matrix with FULL tags (free limit enforced client-side at two, server-side later in step 9); share screen with QR/link and "Play hole 1". Late joiners land on the current hole.
- **Join by link** (Pass 6 §2): universal link `xix.golf/r/{code}` → `joinable_rows` → claim → hole card. The no-app landing page is a static page in `web/` deployed to `xix.golf` (any static host); it shows the round and the App Store / Open links.
- **Round menu and End round** (Pass 7 §1).
- **Results screen** (Pass 5 5e, Pass 6 6c): headline score and games line, medals as riso patches (static SVG/PNG set for the five silhouettes with text overlays), per-game outcomes, callouts with SIGNED/DUCK IT marks, stickers received, highlights, Rival Points small. Share/export via the renderer.
- **Result-link confirm** (Pass 6 §2): results by link → Sign in with Apple → `confirm_round` (+ `claim_row` if needed).

*Acceptance:* two phones, one round, from "New round" to results, including a guest joining by link mid-round. Recorded as a video.

### Step 2 — Stickers on Rive

- Rive runtime via SPM. One `.riv` per sticker with a state machine: `pinned` (still) and `played` (the slap: peel in at 1.4× / −34°, smack at frame 3 with the cue, squash 0.82×1.18, rebound 1.08×0.92, settle flat, ~620 ms). Assets produced from the 1024 masters in the Rive editor; the 512 bundle versions generated by script (Doc 2 note).
- Pinned stickers on the grid and hole card switch from PNG stills to the Rive `pinned` state (visually identical); tapping a sticker you received plays `played` full-screen and calls `session.markPlayed`.
- The **callout banner's** SIGN IT / DUCK IT pressed state moves into the CALLED_OUT `.riv` (darken + 0.96), hit areas unchanged.
- Sound: one slap cue, respects the Settings toggle and the silent switch.
- Sticker packs: `pack_key` on stickers; `base` free; pack entitlement stub reads a local set until StoreKit in step 9.

*Acceptance:* every one of the twelve plays its slap on device at 60 fps; a received sticker tapped on device A notifies device B ("Dave opened your YIKES").

### Step 3 — Callout composer

- From the hole card: "Call someone out" → composer sheet: kind (Target / Duel / Partner / Double), targets (one or crew), goal for Target (par / birdie / a number), partner pick for Partner, the game for Double; preview of the banner; Send → `create_callout`. Rejected if any participant has scored the hole (server) with the toast.
- Free tier gate stub (step 9 replaces it).

*Acceptance:* all four kinds created on device A, responded per-target on B and C, resolved by the engine, shown on results.

### Step 4 — Where to now (MMM)

- Migration 0018: a read-only view `xix.where_to_now(lat, lng, category)` over MMM's tables, filtered to eat/drink, ordered by MMM's own ranking, exposing exactly the fields the MMM feed renders (name, city, illustration, drive minutes/km, open status, the user's like/bookmark state). Entitlement evaluated by MMM's rules inside the view (free allowance, Insider). Coordinated with the MMM schema owner before the migration lands — this is the only place XIX touches MMM.
- `WhereToNowScreen` reproduces the MMM feed as built (Pass 5 5f, Pass 6 6h): "WHERE TO NOW?" line, MMM header with sort chevrons, Eat/Drink pills, hero card, two-column grid, status dots and lines, MMM empty tiles, yellow "Open in My Main Man" handoff (deep link; App Store fallback). Locked state is MMM's Insider card. MMM's design tokens are imported for this screen only (a small `MMMFeedKit` package extracted from MMM, or a faithful copy pinned to MMM's version).
- Course coordinates come from `courses` (OSM enrichment job in step 8) or from the device location at round end with permission.

*Acceptance:* an MMM Insider sees the full feed in XIX; a non-Insider hits MMM's gate with MMM's card; the handoff opens MMM to the same place.

### Step 5 — Cabinet, rivalry, rankings, crews

- Cabinet and medal context card (Pass 6 6d, Pass 7 §3): patches grid by season; context card with the round's exported card via the renderer; share.
- Rivalry view: head-to-head from `results`/`medals` between two profiles; last five rounds with the stickers thrown.
- Rankings (Pass 6 6e): `ranking_entries` materialised by a nightly cron function (Stableford vs Level, birdies this month, rounds played, Rival Points; global and per crew); pending row with Confirm / I object → `confirm_round`.
- Crews (Pass 7 §4): create, invite link, board.

*Acceptance:* after three rounds across two accounts, both rivalry and both boards show correct numbers against a hand tally.

### Step 6 — First run, settings, notifications, inbox

- First run solo (Pass 6 6i); Settings (Pass 6 6j) including quiet mode default and Level display.
- Push notifications via APNs through a Supabase edge function triggered by callouts, sticker played, results ready, round invite (Pass 7 §2, ink-only copy). Device tokens in a new `xix.devices` table. In-app inbox.

*Acceptance:* the four notification types arrive on a locked phone with the Pass 7 copy; tapping each opens the right screen.

### Step 7 — Snap the card

- Capture screen (Pass 6 6k): frame overlay, name rows before the shutter, Enter by hand / Import shot.
- Recognition: OM's receipt pipeline adapted for handwritten grids (on-device model first; server fallback as an edge function). Output: per-cell strokes with confidence, par row if present, written totals.
- "Check it" confirmation grid: low-confidence cells in amber, written vs read totals, the mismatch line, every cell opens the hole-card pad; confirm → `enter_score` for all cells → results. Poor read → blank grid at par.
- Screenshot import uses the same path.

*Acceptance:* ten real paper cards from the validation weekends; the confirmation grid catches every misread before results.

### Step 8 — Watch, course enrichment, export polish

- watchOS target (Pass 6 6l, Pass 7 §2): own-row entry with the crown, in-play glance, incoming callout poster with SIGN IT / DUCK IT, SIGNED confirmation, complication. Sync through the phone (WatchConnectivity → session).
- OSM enrichment job: for courses with an `osm_id` or a confident name+region match, fill `course_holes` par/SI/yards and hole geometry; hole silhouettes optional on the card.
- Export: names/initials toggle, join link/QR corner, share sheet; results image.

*Acceptance:* a full round scored from the wrist on one player's watch while the others use phones.

### Step 9 — Paywall and packs (StoreKit 2)

- Products: `xix.full.annual`, `xix.full.monthly`, sticker packs as consumables/non-consumables per pack. Paywall (Pass 6 6g) and the two contextual entry points. Server-side entitlement check via App Store Server Notifications into `xix.entitlements`; RPCs for `set_games` (third game) and `create_callout` enforce it.
- Restore purchases; sandbox tested; no countdown, no urgency copy.

*Acceptance:* sandbox purchase unlocks the third game and callouts on device; server-side enforcement refuses without entitlement.

### Step 10 — TestFlight

- App icon, launch screen, App Store metadata (category Sports, keyword "golf scorecard", screenshots from the Pass 5 proof states), privacy nutrition labels, App Privacy policy URL on xix.golf.
- Crash reporting (MetricKit + a lightweight service), analytics events from Doc 2, debug menu removed from Release.
- 512px sticker bundle, masters in `masters/`.
- Legal review checklist: contest rules (none in V1), guideline 5.3 language, vocabulary sweep across metadata.

*Acceptance:* TestFlight build installed on four phones; one full round played by the validation group; results screen screenshotted and posted to their group chat without prompting.
